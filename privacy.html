1. Who we are

ShyftRota is scheduling software for shift-based teams, operated by ShyftRota ("ShyftRota", "we", "us"), based in San Diego, California, United States.

For questions about this policy or your information, email hello@shyftrota.com.

2. Two kinds of data

It matters which of these applies to you, because your rights are exercised differently.

  • Data we control. Information from visitors to this website and from people who contact us or sign their business up, such as a pilot request. We decide how this is used, and you can bring requests about it straight to us.
  • Data we process for a customer. Information inside a customer's ShyftRota account, such as employee records, schedules, time punches and messages. The property or business that bought ShyftRota decides what goes in and how it is used. We handle it on their instructions. If you are an employee at one of those businesses, see section 10.

3. What we collect

Website visitors

This website has no advertising trackers, no analytics pixels and no third-party marketing scripts. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, including IP address, browser type and the pages requested, which are used to keep the site running and secure.

Pilot requests and other enquiries

When you send the pilot form or email us, we receive your name, work email address, the name of your property or business, your role, roughly how many employees you schedule, how many locations you run, and anything you choose to write in the message field.

4. How we use it

  • To provide the service: building schedules, recording hours, delivering messages and notifications.
  • To send transactional email, such as a schedule being posted, a reminder before a shift, and unread messages. These are part of the service rather than marketing.
  • To reply to pilot requests and support questions.
  • To keep accounts secure, investigate misuse and meet legal obligations.
  • To understand which features are used and fix what is broken, using aggregated information rather than individual profiles.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not use customer data to train machine learning models.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on performance of a contract for providing the service, legitimate interests for security and product improvement, and consent where consent is required.

6. Cookies and local storage

This marketing website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts.

The ShyftRota application stores a small amount of data in your browser so the product works:

  • Session tokens, which keep you signed in. Signing out clears them.
  • Preferences, such as your light or dark theme choice and your selected industry.

These are strictly necessary for the service to function, so no cookie banner is shown. You can clear them in your browser at any time, though you will be signed out.

7. How long we keep it

  • Pilot requests and enquiries: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Account and scheduling data: for as long as the customer's account is active.
  • After an account closes: deleted or anonymised within 90 days, except where we must keep records for legal, tax or accounting reasons.
  • Server and security logs: typically up to 12 months.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed, never stored in readable form. All traffic runs over HTTPS. Access to each property's data is enforced at the database level by row-level security policies, so one property's staff cannot read another property's records. Administrative access to production systems is limited to people who need it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify affected customers and any regulator the law requires, without undue delay.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Receive your information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent where we relied on it.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

California residents. Under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, you have the rights above, including the right to know, delete, and correct. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined, so there is nothing to opt out of. You may use an authorised agent to make a request.

How to make a request. Email hello@shyftrota.com with the subject "Data request". We will verify who you are, usually by replying to the email address on the account, and respond within 45 days. If you have a ShyftRota login, you can also delete your own account from within the app.

If you are in the EEA or UK and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

10. If you work at a customer property

Your employer decides what goes into ShyftRota and how it is used. They are the controller of that information and we handle it for them.

Send requests to see, correct or delete your records to your manager or your employer's HR contact first. If you contact us directly, we will pass the request to them and help them answer it, but we cannot change or delete an employer's records without their instruction.

11. Children

ShyftRota is a workplace tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. Employers are responsible for complying with the rules that apply to scheduling workers under 18, including any restrictions on their hours. If you believe a child's information has reached us in error, email us and we will remove it.

12. International transfers

We operate in the United States, and our providers store data there. If you use ShyftRota from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from your own. Where required, we use Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful transfer mechanism.

13. Changes

We may update this policy as the product changes. The effective date at the top always reflects the current version. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we will email account holders before it takes effect.
