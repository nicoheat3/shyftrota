How it works

Your numbers in. A covered week out.

No spreadsheet, no group text, no guessing how many people Saturday needs.

01

Enter the volume

Type each day's arrivals, due outs and occupancy, the numbers you already pull from the PMS. ShyftRota turns them into how many people you need on AM, Mid and PM.

FAQ

Questions GMs ask.

Do my employees need to download an app?

No. ShyftRota runs in the phone's browser, so there's nothing to install from an app store. Staff can add it to their home screen so it opens like an app.

Where do the volume numbers come from?

You enter each day's arrivals, due outs and occupancy, usually straight from your property management system's forecast. ShyftRota uses them to work out how many people you need on each shift.

Does it replace our payroll system?

No. Staff clock in and out in ShyftRota, and you export the hours as a CSV for Paychex, ADP, Gusto or another provider.

How are lunch breaks handled?

Every shift over five hours counts a 30-minute unpaid lunch, so a 7:00a–3:30p shift is 8 paid hours toward each person's weekly max.

Can staff at one hotel see another hotel's schedule?

No. Each property's data is kept separate, and staff only see their own property.
