FAQ

Questions managers ask.

Do my employees need to download an app?

No. ShyftRota runs in the phone's browser, so there's nothing to install from an app store. Staff can add it to their home screen so it opens like an app.

Is this only for hotels?

No. The scheduling, swaps, time clock, chat and payroll export work for any team that runs on shifts, and ShyftRota ships with role presets for hotels, restaurants, healthcare, retail and general teams. One feature is hotel-specific: setting your daily cover from arrivals, due outs and occupancy. Hotels are where it's been proven week to week, so that's the version I can show you working today.

Where do the volume numbers come from?

You type in each day's arrivals, due outs and occupancy, the numbers you already read off your morning report. There's no integration with your property management system, so nothing has to be connected before you start. You set the thresholds for your own property.

Does it replace our payroll system?

No. Staff clock in and out in ShyftRota, and you export the hours as a CSV to bring into Paychex, ADP, Gusto or another provider. ShyftRota doesn't calculate pay or file anything.

How are lunch breaks handled?

Every shift over five hours counts a 30-minute unpaid lunch, so a 7:00a–3:30p shift is 8 paid hours toward each person's weekly max. ShyftRota is a scheduling tool, not a compliance service, so you stay responsible for meeting your own break and overtime rules.

Can staff at one property see another property's schedule?

No. Each property's data is separated in the database, and staff only see their own property.
