Features

Everything the schedule
demands. Nothing extra.

Built from real experience on the floor — not a product roadmap in a boardroom.

✦

AI Schedule Generation

One tap fills the entire week around your team's availability, max hours, and approved time off. Employees on PTO are skipped automatically. Adjust any cell manually — the AI is a starting point, not a mandate.

🕐

Time Clock + Payroll Export

Employees clock in and out from the app. Admins see live who's on the floor. Export a Paychex, ADP, or Gusto-ready CSV with one tap — including lunch breaks.

🔄

Shift Swaps & Open Shifts

Employees request swaps and see each other's shifts before submitting. Admins post open shifts and see exactly who's free to fill them.

🏖

PTO Management

Full date-range picker for time-off requests. Approved PTO automatically blocks scheduling — no double-booking.

🏢

Departments & Cross-Team View

Organize staff into departments. Link teams that work together — Front Desk and Housekeeping can see each other's daily rosters in a clean, restricted view.

💬

Direct Messages + Team Chat

Built-in group channel and 1-on-1 direct messages between any two people. No sharing personal numbers. Unread badges so nothing gets missed. Managers reach anyone instantly.

📞

Call-In Notices

Employees notify you of absences, late arrivals, or early departures right in the app. No more frantic texts at 6am.

🔒

Schedule History

Past weeks lock automatically as permanent, read-only records. Navigate back any week and see exactly what was scheduled.
